Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Romeo's Pizza.

Where are Romeo's Pizza locations?

Romeo's Pizza has 8 locations: 3 in Abuja (Garki, Gwarinpa, Lugbe) and 5 in Lagos (Yaba, Surulere, Okota, Festac, Ajao Estate). Visit the Locations page for more information.

What are your operating hours?

All Romeo's Pizza stores are open Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Can I schedule an order for later?

Yes. When placing your order online, select the Scheduled Order option and choose your preferred delivery or pickup time.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes between 25 to 30 minutes, depending on your location and order size. During peak hours, delivery may take longer.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery is free within a 3KM radius of any Romeo's Pizza store. Orders outside this radius may attract a delivery fee based on distance.

What payment methods do you accept?

Romeo's Pizza accepts card payments, including major credit and debit cards. Delivery riders carry POS machines for payment on delivery.

Where can I place an order?

You can place an order through the Romeo's Pizza website , by calling 07048683613, or through delivery platforms such as ChowDeck and Glovo.
